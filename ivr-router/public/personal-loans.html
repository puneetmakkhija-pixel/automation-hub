Not shown here, and why

Delivered and read counts start from 2 Sep. /webhooks/ananta used to log each delivery receipt and discard it — roughly 6,700 a day. It now records them, but only from the moment that change deployed: every press before it has no receipt and never will. A zero on an older day is an absence of records, not an absence of deliveries, which is why the cards show how many sends actually carry a receipt rather than a percentage.

Lender MIS outcomes. There is no key to join a press to a lender outcome. crm.mis_poon holds 2,051 rows of which 14 carry a phone number and none carry a client reference, and its newest MIS date is 26 Aug — before these campaigns ran. crm.mis_hero holds one row. The durable fix is upstream: the customer_id already minted for every presser should ride the lender link so it comes back in the MIS as the client reference.
